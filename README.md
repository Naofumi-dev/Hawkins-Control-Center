### Inbox, agents, and sending behavior

The sync commits its Gmail history checkpoint only after all pages and message operations succeed. Initial full sync anchors history before listing; the next run catches arrivals during the scan. An expired checkpoint causes a full retained-window rescan plus rechecking previously stored message IDs, so missed deletion/Spam/Trash events revoke stale context. Partial writes deduplicate on retry and never advance the failed checkpoint. The backend has a 30-minute sync lease; exceptionally large imports must be observed for lease/HTTP timeouts and rerun safely. Google documents full/partial synchronization and expired history recovery in its [sync contract](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/guides/sync).

Will handles ambiguous classification and request extraction; Eleven prepares a brief; Steve prepares reply/Loom/proposal text; Hopper creates exact-revision review tasks/cards; Max queues an internal reminder after five weekdays; Mike's dispatcher coordinates leases, dependencies, retries, and recorded status; Joyce prepares onboarding only after an owner-confirmed accepted agreement. Model-backed tasks save inputs by message/context references and store outputs, evidence, blockers, and attempts. Hopper, Max's eligibility, and Mike's coordination use deterministic rules rather than unnecessary model calls. Failed/uncertain paid runs are not automatically retried. Each retry requires a fresh cost reservation. Agent drafts are suggestions until copied into and saved as a reviewed message revision.

OpenRouter requests use `anthropic/claude-haiku-4.5`, at most 24 KB of context and 1,200 output tokens, strict structured output, supported-parameter routing, no provider fallback, and a price ceiling. The published [model price](https://openrouter.ai/anthropic/claude-haiku-4.5), rechecked 2026-10-05, is $1/M input and $5/M output tokens for standard endpoints. The existing atomic cost ledger reserves a conservative maximum before each attempt and shares the **$25/month** cap with Brave. An uncertain charge remains reserved. Personal credentials and real billing remain unverified. Higher-priced endpoints are excluded through [provider routing controls](https://openrouter.ai/docs/guides/routing/provider-selection).

Approved replies use the same immutable message revisions and send-attempt ledger as outreach, with separate buckets: **5 outreach attempts/day** including cold follow-ups, and **20 replies/day** requiring verified inbound correspondence. Canceled, failed, and unknown attempts conservatively consume capacity too. Limits use Asia/Manila. A reply stops cold follow-ups without blocking an approved conversational response. New incoming or manually sent correspondence, edits, or changed classification invalidate pending approval. Gmail replies retain the [thread ID and required headers](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/guides/threads); an OnlineJobs-to-email transition starts a new Gmail email linked to the same opportunity. An uncertain send stays Needs checking. The internal `/replies/reconcile` endpoint checks Sent by its deterministic RFC Message-ID and exact payload; absence of a match does not authorize a resend.

The old workflow 03 still supplies the cold-outreach monitor and stale-attempt sweep. Keep its personal account binding verified. Cold sending continues to require its independent fresh monitor checkpoint; the new history sync does not silently satisfy that older gate. New reply delivery also sweeps stalled attempts before selecting a send. Reply activation, original cold discovery activation, and website deployment are separate setup actions.

- The service requires `CLIENT_FINDER_SERVICE_TOKEN`, `SUPABASE_URL`, `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`, and `BRAVE_SEARCH_API_KEY` for discovery. `/mail-poll` and `/card` do not need Brave. Never paste keys into source or exports.

- The 0.5-cent reservation matches [Brave's published Search price of $5 per 1,000 requests](https://brave.com/search/api/) checked on 2026-09-27. Refresh the price before activation and change the reservation upward if the price increases.
- Brave setup remains pending: its documentation checked on 2026-09-29 permits $0 prepayment with a card and includes $5 monthly credits, but retaining API results requires explicit storage rights. Free credits alone do not establish those rights.
- The SQL functions reserve a **shared** limit of five initial drafts/day and five cold-outreach email attempts/day in Asia/Manila. Follow-ups and unresolved attempts count toward the send limit. - A completed, gap-free inbox scan within 30 minutes is required to claim or begin a send. An unresolved inbox item or provider error pauses sending. Clear the pause reason only after manual investigation.
- The workflows disable routine success/error execution-payload saving because records can contain recipient addresses, full email bodies, or inbound messages. Keep access to n8n, Supabase, and host logs limited to the owner.
